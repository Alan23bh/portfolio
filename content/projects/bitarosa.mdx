### A responsive WordPress website built for a real small-business client, focused on clear service navigation, mobile usability, and dependable contact workflows.

---

## Overview

Bitarosa is a service-based website built for a client offering hair, nail, and
event services. The goal was to create a polished online presence that clearly
communicated each service while making it easy for potential customers to find
information and get in touch.

The project involved direct client communication, iterative updates, responsive
design work, contact-form setup, and production troubleshooting.

---

## The problem

The business needed a website that could bring several services together
without making the experience confusing. Visitors needed to understand what was
offered, move through the site easily on mobile devices, and reach the client
through a dependable contact flow.

During production, I also had to work through routing, publishing, and caching
issues that affected how the live site behaved.

---

## The solution

I built the site in WordPress using Astra, Spectra, and WPForms. I organized the
content around the client's service categories, created responsive layouts, and
configured contact forms for lead capture.

I worked directly with the client to implement feedback, improve navigation,
and make updates based on changing business needs.

---

## What I built

• Responsive WordPress layouts for desktop and mobile  
• Clear navigation across multiple service categories  
• WPForms contact and lead-capture workflows  
• Client-provided content and imagery integrated into the site  
• Updates based on direct client feedback  
• Production troubleshooting for routing, publishing, and caching behavior

---

## Production troubleshooting

A meaningful part of this project was learning that a site can look correct in
the editor and still behave differently after publishing. I worked through
routing and caching problems to make the live experience more reliable across
devices.

That process helped me understand how WordPress hosting, caching, and publishing
behavior can affect production websites beyond the page-builder interface.

---

## Result

The finished site gives the business a dedicated online presence where visitors
can learn about its services and contact the client through the website. It also
served as my first substantial experience delivering updates directly from
client feedback and resolving issues on a live WordPress site.

I do not attach unverified traffic or conversion metrics to this project; the
value I can confidently describe is the completed client delivery, production
problem-solving, and direct collaboration involved.

---

## Lessons learned

Bitarosa reinforced that successful client work is not just about writing code
or choosing a theme. Clear communication, mobile usability, reliable forms, and
production troubleshooting matter just as much as the visual design.

It also gave me experience adapting to a client's preferences instead of
treating the project like a personal design exercise.
